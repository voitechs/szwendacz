Szwendacz map patch v1.1

Based on the current GitHub main structure.

Replace/add:
- package.json
- src/components/EuropeProjectMap.astro
- src/pages/index.astro
- src/pages/projects/crown-of-europe.astro
- src/styles/global.css

What changes:
- map geometry comes from Natural Earth 1:50m through world-atlas
- much more detailed country outlines
- climbed countries are filled green
- planned countries are warm gold
- unclimbed project countries are grey
- non-project/background countries are lighter
- countries are clickable and open their country page
- microstates get small clickable markers so they remain usable
- no Leaflet, map tiles, or runtime web requests

New npm dependencies:
- world-atlas 2.0.2
- topojson-client 3.1.0
